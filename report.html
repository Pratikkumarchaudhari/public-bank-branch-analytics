How to explain the analysis

  • Source: a deterministic synthetic CSV in GitHub, generated by Python. It is not a real FDIC download or employer dataset.
  • Platform: Python/Pandas performs key validation, aggregation, joining, cohort comparison, and result generation. NumPy supports numeric validation. This standalone HTML/JavaScript page displays the saved results.
  • Record grain: one branch_id in one year. Repeated IDs across years are expected; duplicate IDs within a year are rejected.
  • Comparison periods: the two latest available years, shown explicitly. They may not be consecutive in another input.
  • Entering / exiting: these describe presence in the dataset, not proven branch openings or closures. Source coverage and identifier changes require investigation.
  • Undefined growth: a zero prior-period denominator produces no percentage. Entering/exiting IDs also have no individual matched-growth percentage.
  • Interpretation: shares and HHI describe the included synthetic sample. They do not establish real market conditions, inflation-adjusted growth, or regulatory findings.
  • Refresh: pipeline execution now rebuilds the CSV/JSON outputs and HTML report together. This published sample is a batch snapshot.
